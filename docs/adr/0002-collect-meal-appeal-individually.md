# Collect meal appeal individually

The pilot will present one meal at a time and ask “If this meal were ready to eat and available to you now, would you eat it?” with Yes, No, and Not sure responses. All three models will be compared on definite Yes/No responses; Not sure will be retained separately and will not be converted to No. This measures appetite assuming availability while allowing several meals to be appealing, without turning practical barriers or unchosen alternatives into negative appeal labels.
