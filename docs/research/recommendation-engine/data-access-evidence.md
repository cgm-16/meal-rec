# Obtainable external data and defensible joins

Checked 2026-10-17. Research only; Ori has **no existing purchase-data access**. No accounts, credentials, personal records, or large datasets were accessed. This note distinguishes documented fields from proposed uses; a documented API is not an available MealRec dataset.

**Recommendation:** weather is obtainable now as a contextual feature, but needs MealRec outcomes and location/time records to establish value. For dish-level history, voluntary itemized order exports or receipts are a more concrete discovery route than ordinary bank transactions. Merchant APIs become realistic only with an actual participating merchant. Public grocery datasets support methodological experiments; they do not supply linked MealRec-user preference labels.

## What the joins can and cannot establish

**Public identities cannot be turned into MealRec people.** Anonymous grocery households and Food.com accounts are separate populations and item spaces. Product-name or ingredient overlap can suggest content features or priors, but supplies no same-person link. Buying noodles, sauce and vegetables does not label a particular dish, its preparation date, who ate it, or whether anyone liked it. These are inferences we should test, not dataset facts.

**Weather needs point-in-time discipline.** Retrospective reanalysis is useful for studying conditions, but contains information reconstructed after the event; ERA5/ERA5-Land are listed with a [five-day delay](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api). For a recommendation made before lunch, actual afternoon rain or the final daily maximum is unavailable. Proposed evidence rule: store the response available at serving time, request time, location granularity, model/version when available, forecast initialization and valid time. For backtests, require a run actually published before the decision; initialization alone does not prove availability. Use the forecast horizon the product would have had. The stitched historical forecast is not a shortcut around this requirement. These are recommended safeguards, inferred from the documented archive constructions.

**Purchases label behavior under constraints.** Promotions, stock, price, delivery fees, household sharing, group orders, refunds and ordering for someone else can all separate purchase from desire. Square even documents that [offline POS orders may arrive up to 72 hours later](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/orders-api/search-orders), with server `created_at` reflecting transmission time. Export omissions can conceal relevant constraints. None of the checked sources supplies MealRec's rendered alternatives or outside option.

**Strongest counterargument:** broad external histories might still improve cold-start priors or routine/restaurant prediction, and itemized merchant data clearly can contain dishes. That is a credible hypothesis. Its value depends on accessible records, target/item alignment and improvement on MealRec outcomes; millions of grocery events do not resolve those dependencies. A modest data partnership could eventually outweigh direct app history, but no such access is established today.

## Practical next evidence

1. Establish intended country, food setting and target: dish chosen, ordered, eaten or positively rated. Do not infer the deployment market from Ori's current timezone.
2. With separately obtained participant permission, inspect a small set of voluntarily supplied **redacted** receipts/order exports from that market. Do not request account passwords. Measure usable item/time/place coverage, repeat-history depth, individual attribution, cancellation/refund handling and the fraction confidently mappable to MealRec's 91 seed meals. Keep uncertain mappings explicit.
3. Establish the actual rights and operational access before any dataset/model reuse: Instacart authorized download and commercial eligibility; dunnhumby current license/download; Food.com release terms; merchant consent/Orders permissions or aggregator Production/institution eligibility; export import permissions and format.
4. In parallel, obtain prospective MealRec decision/outcome records with coarse location and the weather actually available at recommendation time. Then compare the same outcome prediction with and without weather, and later any validated external history. Weather access alone does not justify wiring in a positive score.

No implementation, authenticated API call, paid access or legal determination was performed.
