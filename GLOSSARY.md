# MealRec

MealRec helps people discover meals that appeal to them at the moment they are deciding what to eat.

## Language

**Immediate appeal**:
A person's willingness to eat a particular meal now, assuming it is ready and available to eat. It reflects current appetite and taste, independent of obtaining, affording, or preparing the meal.
_Avoid_: Preference (without specifying whether it is general or immediate)

**Appeal response**:
A person's explicit Yes, No, or Not sure answer about one meal's immediate appeal at the moment it is presented. Yes and No express definite answers; Not sure expresses uncertainty.
_Avoid_: Rating (without specifying whether it concerns immediate appeal or general preference)

**Weather city**:
The city a participant selects to represent their current location for the pilot's weather context. It can change between visits.
_Avoid_: Home city (when the participant is elsewhere)

**Hunger state**:
A participant's self-reported hunger at the start of a pilot visit: Not hungry, Somewhat hungry, or Very hungry. A skipped answer leaves hunger unknown.
_Avoid_: Appetite (when it is unclear whether hunger or meal appeal is meant)

**Meal exposure**:
A meal presented to a pilot participant at a particular time and in a particular context. Presenting the same meal again creates another exposure, whether or not the participant answers.
_Avoid_: Appeal response (when referring to a presentation without an answer)

**Eligible meal**:
A meal allowed by a participant's declared dietary exclusions for the pilot.
_Avoid_: Recommended meal (eligibility does not establish appeal)
