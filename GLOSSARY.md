# MealRec

MealRec helps people discover meals that appeal to them at the moment they are deciding what to eat.

## Language

**Immediate appeal**:
A person's willingness to eat a particular meal at the time of a recommendation. It can change with their circumstances even when their general tastes remain the same.
_Avoid_: Preference (without specifying whether it is general or immediate)

**Appeal response**:
A person's explicit answer about a meal's immediate appeal at the moment it is presented.
_Avoid_: Rating (without specifying whether it concerns immediate appeal or general preference)
